specific, and judged by an AI judge in three blind rounds.

> One star. He filled my bowl halfway, called it dinner, then ate an entire pizza in front of me. I have contacted the dog.

## Entry

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the single line above to `/output/entry.txt` — one line,
121 characters, under the 200-character cap.
